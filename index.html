NYU Langone Health — Lang Lab

Research Assistant · May 2026–Present

I collaborate directly with Eric Lang, MD, PhD on a scikit-image pipeline for large-scale analysis of animal cerebellum histology, enabling analyses previously impractical at dataset scale.

I also partner with Esma Cetinkaya, PhD and Billur Avilar, PhD on behavioral research spaces and experimental rigs.
